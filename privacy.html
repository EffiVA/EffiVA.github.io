Privacy Policy

Clinic Bookings · Effective October 1, 2026 · Last updated October 1, 2026

Clinic Bookings ("the app", "we", "us") helps clinics manage appointment bookings using Google Sheets. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By signing in with Google and using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Clinic Bookings is operated by Lance Refugio. You can reach us at lanz.acads@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

Information we access

Google account information. When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture. We use these to identify you and show who is signed in.

Google Sheets data. With your permission, the app reads and writes to Google Sheets spreadsheets that you choose or that the app creates for you. This is where appointment records are kept. Depending on how a clinic uses the app, those records can include:

  • Patient or client name and contact details (such as phone number or email)
  • Appointment date, time and service type
  • Booking status and any notes entered by clinic staff

The app does not access your Gmail, Google Drive files other than the spreadsheets described above, contacts, or any other Google data.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your session active.
  • To create, display, edit and cancel appointment bookings in your spreadsheet.
  • To let clinic staff view and manage the schedule.
  • To respond to support requests and keep the app working.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles or train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Clinic Bookings' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the user-facing booking features described above.

Where your data is stored

Appointment data lives in Google Sheets in the Google account of the person or clinic that owns the spreadsheet. We do not copy this data to our own servers or maintain a separate database of it. Data you enter is subject to Google's own security and privacy practices.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only:

  • With Google, as needed for the app to work through Google's APIs.
  • With people you give access to the spreadsheet (for example, clinic staff).
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the public.

Health and sensitive information

Clinic Bookings is a scheduling tool. It is not designed to store medical records, diagnoses, treatment details or other clinical information. Clinics should not enter detailed health information into booking notes. Each clinic is responsible for how it handles information it collects from its patients and for complying with the health privacy laws that apply to it.

Security

Sign-in uses Google's OAuth 2.0, so the app never sees or stores your Google password. The app requests only the permissions it needs, and all communication with Google APIs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to spreadsheet data is controlled by the sharing settings you set in Google Sheets. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Retention and deletion

We keep no copy of your appointment data on our own systems. Records stay in your spreadsheet until you or your staff delete them. You can:

  • Delete any record or the whole spreadsheet directly in Google Sheets.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Email lanz.acads@gmail.com to ask us to delete any information we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. This includes rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 for users in the Philippines. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information through account sign-in. Clinics that book appointments for minors should do so with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If a change affects how we use Google user data, we will notify users before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email lanz.acads@gmail.com.
