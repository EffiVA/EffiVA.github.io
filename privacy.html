Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Clinic Appointment System (the "Application") collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by users when using the application.

1. Information We Collect

The Application may collect information necessary to provide clinic appointment and queue management services. This may include:

  • Name
  • Contact information, such as phone number or email address
  • Appointment date and time
  • Appointment and queue information
  • Other information voluntarily provided by the user through the application

2. Google Account Information

If the Application requests access to Google services, it may access Google Account information or Google service data only to the extent necessary to provide the application's functionality.

Google user data will not be sold, rented, or used for advertising. Google user data will only be used for the purposes disclosed in this Privacy Policy and to provide the features of the Application.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the Application may be used to:

  • Create and manage clinic appointments
  • Manage appointment queues
  • Display appointment-related information
  • Maintain and operate the appointment system
  • Respond to appointment-related requests or inquiries
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Application

4. Google User Data

If Google user data is accessed, it will only be used to provide or improve features that are directly related to the functionality of the Application.

The Application does not sell Google user data, use Google user data for targeted advertising, or transfer Google user data to data brokers. Google user data will not be used for purposes unrelated to the Application's functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information or Google user data. Information may only be shared when necessary to operate the Application, provide requested services, comply with applicable laws, or protect the security and rights of the Application and its users.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored using services and systems used to operate the Application. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services of the Application, maintain appointment records, or comply with applicable legal or operational requirements.

Users may request deletion or correction of their personal information by contacting the Application administrator using the contact information provided below. Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or authorization where such consent is required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's data practices or functionality changes. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, please contact the Application administrator.
