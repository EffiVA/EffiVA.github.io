Privacy Policy for Clinic Bookings

What we access

With your permission, Clinic Bookings accesses Google Sheets to read and write appointment data.

How we use it

Data is used only to provide the booking features of the app. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

Data stays in the clinic's Google spreadsheet. We do not copy it to any other server. It is kept only as long as the clinic keeps it there.

Deletion

To request deletion of your data, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com. You can also revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
